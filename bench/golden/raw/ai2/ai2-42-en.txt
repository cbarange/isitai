To file an income tax return without avoidable errors, start by collecting every document before opening any form. Gather salary statements, bank interest summaries, invoices for deductible work and proof of any credits you intend to claim. Create one folder, physical or digital, and place each document in it as it arrives rather than sorting later. Obtain last year's return, because it lists the categories you used and the accounts you declared. Enter income first and check the total against the sum of the statements before touching any deduction. Add deductions one at a time, confirming the ceiling for each category, since caps differ and are rarely obvious. If a credit applies, verify whether it reduces the tax or the base, because the two are not interchangeable. Run the estimate, compare it with the withholding already paid, and note the direction of the difference. Review the completed form against the document folder line by line before submitting. Save a copy of the submitted return and of every supporting document, then diarise the retention period.
