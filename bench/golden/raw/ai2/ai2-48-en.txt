To secure your accounts, begin by listing every service that holds personal or financial data. Install a password manager and create a master passphrase made of several unrelated words rather than a single clever string. Enable multi-factor authentication on the email account first, because email resets everything else. Replace passwords in order of risk, starting with banking, email and any account linked to payments. Let the manager generate long random passwords and never reuse one across two services. Store recovery codes offline, printed or on a device that is not your phone, and label them without naming the accounts. Remove accounts you no longer use rather than leaving dormant credentials behind. Review the list of active sessions and revoke anything you do not recognise. Set the manager to lock automatically and to require the master passphrase after a short idle period. Revisit the whole list once a year, and immediately after any breach notification.
