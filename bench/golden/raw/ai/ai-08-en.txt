The project status meeting took place on Tuesday morning over video, with six of the eight invited people attending. The agenda covered progress on workstream two, delivery delays and preparation for user acceptance testing. The project lead opened by confirming that the production date stays at the end of the month, with no further slack available. The lead developer reported that three of five screens are complete and that the remaining two depend on design sign-off. The infrastructure supplier flagged a two-day delay in provisioning the test environments, which pushes the load tests back. After some discussion the group agreed to start testing on a reduced environment and migrate afterwards. The quality lead asked for a feature freeze from the twenty-fifth so that test scenarios can stabilise. Two actions were recorded: design approval before Thursday and environment delivery before Friday. The next meeting is set for the same slot the following week.
