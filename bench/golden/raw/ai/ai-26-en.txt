Reorganising a kitchen should not require moving house. Our home organising service comes to you for a single day, working from a plan built on how you actually cook rather than on a catalogue. We start by watching where you stand, where things get dumped and what makes you lose time. Then we propose storage that fits the cabinets you already own, with no building work and no new countertop. Modular bins, drawer dividers and airtight containers are included in the flat fee. By the end of the day your kitchen is rearranged, duplicates are sorted and you know where everything lives. We leave a photographed plan taped inside one cupboard so the whole household can follow it. The price is fixed, quoted in advance, and covers materials. A month later we come back free of charge to adjust whatever is not working.
