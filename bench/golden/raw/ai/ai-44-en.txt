An outage lasting several hours hit the online services of several regional companies on Tuesday, with no attack confirmed at this stage. The problem surfaced late in the morning, when users reported connections dropping after a few minutes. The incident was first blamed on the network provider, before technical teams pointed to an intermediate device. According to a network administrator who followed the case, the device in question closed idle sessions earlier than the applications expected. The fix consisted of sending a regular signal to keep connections open. No personal data was exposed, management said, adding that logs had been retained. The staff union is calling for an audit of configuration timeouts across the whole estate. Incidents of this kind are common and rarely dramatic, which helps explain how little coverage they get. The companies involved say they intend to review testing procedures before any further update.
