My kitchen is small, and for a long time I assumed the problem was simply a lack of space. In truth I was losing time walking around badly placed things. One Sunday I emptied every cupboard onto the table, which forced me to look at what I actually owned. I found three identical peelers and a food processor I had used exactly once. I kept what I use weekly and gave the rest away without much sentiment. Then I put plates next to the dishwasher, pans under the hob and knives exactly where my hand reaches first. The single most useful change was a plain box for lids, which used to live in every drawer at once. These days tidying up after dinner takes ten minutes, and I no longer feel like I am fighting my own counter. I did not expect a drawer to change my mood, but the utensil one did.
