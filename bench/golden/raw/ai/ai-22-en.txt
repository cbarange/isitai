Our guided mountain days are built for people who want real altitude without improvising their way up. Every weekend a certified guide takes a group of no more than eight along a route chosen the day before according to weather and ground conditions. The programme stays deliberately flexible, because a closed pass or a passing storm is not something you negotiate with. We provide poles, crampons when the season calls for them, and a hot drink at the top. Pace is set by the slowest walker, which lets beginners join without fear and regulars still enjoy the scenery. The price covers guiding, insurance and transport from the village, with no hidden extras. Departures are at seven sharp, since starting late is the single most common cause of trouble in the mountains. Book online until Thursday evening and we confirm the meeting point the day before. If we cancel for safety reasons you are refunded in full, with nothing to claim.
