The workshop opens its doors on Saturday mornings and you can walk in knowing nothing about clay. We run three-hour sessions on the wheel, on slab building and on glazing. Everyone leaves with two pieces, fired and glazed by us within three weeks. Tools, clay and an apron are provided, so just wear something you do not mind marking. Groups are capped at six so that each person actually gets attention. Children from eight upwards are welcome with an adult. Finished work is also for sale in the shop, thrown and signed by the two potters who run the place. If you are after a gift, course vouchers are available all year and never expire. Come and have a look, the smell of clay does the rest.
