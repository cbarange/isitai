I spent most of last week trying to work out why an application kept dropping its connection every ten minutes. At first I blamed the server, then the ISP, then my own patience. The application log showed nothing useful, just timeouts with no explanation. Reading the packets, I eventually spotted a reset that arrived after exactly the same interval every time. The culprit was an intermediate firewall that dropped idle connections after six hundred seconds. Our client stayed quiet for longer than that whenever it had nothing to send, which was enough for it to look dead. I added a small keepalive message and everything settled down. At the time I was annoyed at losing two days, but the lesson stuck. Now I check idle timeouts on middleboxes before I go looking anywhere else.
