Should AI Be Regulated in Schools?

Introduction:
Artificial Intelligence is now part of daily life in schools. Students use tools like ChatGPT to do homework, write essays, and prepare for exams. Teachers also use AI to create lessons and correct tests. This raises an important question: should AI be regulated in schools? In my opinion, yes, it must be regulated to protect learning and fairness.

Body:
First, unrestricted use of AI can encourage cheating and laziness. If students let AI do all their work, they will not develop critical thinking, writing skills, or creativity. School is meant to help students learn, not just to get good grades. Second, AI is not always reliable. It can make mistakes, invent false information, or show bias. Without guidance, young pupils may believe everything AI says. Regulation would teach them to use it critically and verify sources. Finally, there are problems of privacy and equality. Some AI tools collect personal data, and not all students have equal access to paid versions. Clear rules would protect students and ensure fairness for everyone.

Conclusion:
In conclusion, AI should not be banned, because it is a useful tool for the future. However, it must be strictly regulated in schools. With clear limits and teacher guidance, AI can support education instead of replacing effort and thinking.
