To make a juicy and fragrant roast chicken with herbs serving four to six people, you will need the following ingredients for the bird, the herb compound butter, and the roasting base.

Ingredients:
1 whole free-range chicken, about 1.8 to 2.2 kg, giblets and neck removed, rinsed quickly and thoroughly patted dry inside and out with paper towels
2 teaspoons fine sea salt, plus more to taste, divided for seasoning the cavity and the skin
1 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper, plus more to taste
1 teaspoon smoked paprika or sweet paprika, for golden color
1 large unwaxed lemon, washed well, one half cut into quarters for the cavity and the other half juiced for the butter
1 whole head of garlic, cut horizontally in half to expose the cloves, plus 4 additional peeled cloves, minced very finely
6 sprigs fresh thyme, plus 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves finely chopped
4 sprigs fresh rosemary, plus 1 teaspoon fresh rosemary needles finely chopped
6 sprigs fresh flat-leaf parsley, plus 2 tablespoons fresh parsley finely chopped
8 fresh sage leaves, 4 left whole and 4 finely chopped
100 g unsalted butter, softened at room temperature until very pliable but not melted
2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, plus a little more for drizzling the pan
1 tablespoon Dijon mustard, for depth and to help the skin brown
1 teaspoon lemon zest, finely grated from the juiced half
3 medium yellow onions, peeled and cut into thick 2 cm wedges
4 medium carrots, peeled and cut into 5 cm chunks
500 g baby potatoes, washed and halved if large
250 ml dry white wine or good chicken stock, for deglazing and keeping the drippings from burning
250 ml homemade or low-sodium chicken stock, for basting and for the pan sauce
2 teaspoons all-purpose flour or cornstarch mixed with 2 teaspoons cold water, optional for thickening the jus
Kitchen twine for trussing, and a large roasting pan with a rack or a heavy cast-iron skillet

Steps:
1. Take the chicken out of the refrigerator 45 to 60 minutes before roasting so it comes to room temperature, which helps it cook evenly and stay juicy. Place the oven rack in the lower-middle position and preheat the oven to 220 degrees Celsius, 425 degrees Fahrenheit. Pat the chicken completely dry again, because any surface moisture will steam the skin instead of letting it crisp.
2. Prepare the herb compound butter that will flavor the meat and crisp the skin. In a medium bowl, combine the softened butter with the olive oil, Dijon mustard, lemon juice and lemon zest, the minced garlic, the chopped thyme, rosemary, parsley and sage. Add one teaspoon of sea salt and half a teaspoon of black pepper. Mash with a fork until you obtain a smooth, green-flecked, very aromatic paste. Taste and adjust salt if needed; it should be well seasoned because it has to season a whole bird.
3. Season the cavity generously. Sprinkle half a teaspoon of salt, a quarter teaspoon of pepper and the paprika inside, then stuff the cavity with the quartered lemon, the halved head of garlic, 3 sprigs of thyme, 2 sprigs of rosemary, 3 sprigs of parsley and the 4 whole sage leaves. Do not pack too tightly; air must circulate to cook the bird from the inside. These aromatics will steam gently and perfume the flesh from within.
4. Loosen the skin carefully to insert butter underneath. With clean hands, slide your fingers gently between the skin and the breast meat on both sides, and over the thighs, being careful not to tear the skin.
