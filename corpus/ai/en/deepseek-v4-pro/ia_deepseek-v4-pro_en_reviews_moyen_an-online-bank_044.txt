I’ve been using this online bank for about a year now, and honestly, it’s been a breath of fresh air. I switched from a traditional bank after getting tired of monthly fees and waiting on hold for simple questions. Opening an account online took less than ten minutes, and the app is clean and intuitive. I can check balances, move money, and deposit checks without feeling like I need a tutorial.

I especially appreciate the high-yield savings rate. It’s not life-changing, but it’s nice watching my emergency fund actually grow a little each month instead of just sitting there. Customer service has also been quick and human whenever I reached out through chat. One time I had a suspicious charge, and they froze my card immediately and sent a replacement within days, no runaround.

There are a few minor drawbacks—there are no physical branches, so cash deposits require a workaround—but for my day-to-day banking, it’s perfect. It feels like a bank built for how I actually live now: mostly digital, no hidden fees, and straightforward. I’ve already recommended it to friends and family. If you’re on the fence, I’d say give it a try. It’s been one of the simplest financial decisions I’ve made.
