**Ingredients** (serves 6)

- 1.2 kg (about 2.5 lb) waxy potatoes, such as Yukon Gold, peeled and thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, one halved, one finely minced
- 500 ml (2 cups) whole milk
- 250 ml (1 cup) heavy cream
- 100 g (1 cup) grated Gruyère cheese
- 50 g (1/2 cup) grated Parmesan cheese
- 30 g (2 tbsp) butter, plus extra for greasing
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme

**Steps**

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Butter a 23 × 33 cm (9 × 13 inch) baking dish and rub it with the halved garlic clove.
2. Combine the milk, cream, minced garlic, thyme sprigs, salt, pepper, and nutmeg in a saucepan. Warm over medium heat until steaming, but do not boil. Remove from the heat and discard the thyme.
3. Layer one third of the potato slices in the dish, overlapping them slightly. Sprinkle with a little Gruyère, then repeat with two more layers of potatoes and cheese.
4. Pour the warm cream mixture evenly over the potatoes. Press gently with a spatula so the potatoes are submerged.
5. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 45 minutes.
6. Remove the foil, press the potatoes down again, and sprinkle the Parmesan over the top. Dot with the remaining butter.
7. Bake uncovered for another 30 to 40 minutes, until the top is golden brown and bubbling and a knife slides easily through the center.
8. Let the gratin rest for 10 minutes before serving so it sets and is easier to slice.
