Ingredients: 1 sheet puff pastry, 3 apples, 2 tbsp butter, 3 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp fresh rosemary (chopped), 1 tbsp honey.

Steps:
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Thinly slice the apples.
3. Roll out the pastry on a baking sheet and prick it with a fork.
4. Arrange the apple slices in overlapping rows.
5. Dot with butter, then sprinkle with sugar and rosemary.
6. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until golden and crisp.
7. Drizzle with honey and serve warm.
