Five stars, and I mean it. I bought these hiking boots about six weeks ago for a trip through the Cascades, and they've become the first thing I reach for before any trail.

I'd been wearing a pair of cheap boots that gave me blisters on every hike longer than three miles. These were a bit pricier, and I hesitated, but the fit is what sold me. My heel stays locked in on steep descents, and the toe box has enough room that my feet don't go numb after a long day. I wore them in for only two short walks before the big trip, and I still expected some rubbing. There wasn't any.

The grip on wet rock has impressed me the most. On our second day it rained most of the morning, and the trail turned into a creek bed. I slipped once, but the outsole held, and I never felt like I was sliding out from under myself. The waterproofing also held up. My socks stayed dry through about two hours of wading across a shallow stream.

They're not perfect. They're fairly heavy, so I wouldn't pick them for fast-and-light day hikes, and the laces came loose once on a rough scramble. A quick double knot fixed that.

Still, for longer trips where comfort and stability matter more than shaving ounces, these have earned a permanent spot in my gear closet. If you're tired of blisters and nervous on wet terrain, I'd recommend them without hesitation.
