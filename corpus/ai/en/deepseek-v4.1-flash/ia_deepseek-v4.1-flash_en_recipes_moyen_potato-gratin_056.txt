Ingredients:
2 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and sliced 1/8 inch thick
1 1/2 cups heavy cream
1/2 cup whole milk
2 garlic cloves, minced
3 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened, plus extra for greasing
1 1/2 cups grated Gruyère or sharp white cheddar
1/2 cup finely grated Parmesan
1 teaspoon kosher salt
1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
1/8 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
1 teaspoon chopped fresh thyme leaves

Steps:
Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a 2-quart baking dish with butter. In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine cream, milk, garlic, salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Warm gently until steaming, not boiling. Arrange one-third of potato slices in the dish, overlapping slightly. Pour over one-third of warm cream mixture and scatter with one-third of Gruyère and Parmesan. Repeat layering twice, finishing with remaining cheese. Dot with remaining butter and sprinkle thyme on top. Cover tightly with foil and bake 45 minutes. Remove foil and bake 20 to 25 minutes more, until potatoes are tender when pierced and top is golden and bubbling. Let rest 10 to 15 minutes before serving so the gratin sets. Serves 6 to 8.
