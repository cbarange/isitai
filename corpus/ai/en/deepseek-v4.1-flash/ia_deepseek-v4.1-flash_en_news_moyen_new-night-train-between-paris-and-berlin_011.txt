The overnight rail link between Paris and Berlin, launched in December 2023, has restored a direct sleeper service between the two capitals. Operated by Austria’s ÖBB under its Nightjet brand in cooperation with SNCF and Deutsche Bahn, the train leaves Paris Gare de l’Est in the evening and reaches Berlin Hauptbahnhof the following morning, with stops including Strasbourg and Frankfurt. The journey takes around 13 to 14 hours and offers seated coaches, couchettes and sleeping cars. The service began with three departures a week in each direction, a frequency that reflected rolling-stock and path constraints; operators have presented it as part of a wider European revival of night trains. Tickets are sold through the participating railways and Nightjet. The link is intended to offer an alternative to short-haul flights and daytime rail journeys that require changes. Passenger groups have welcomed the connection but say more frequent service and better coordination of cross-border ticketing would be needed to make overnight rail a mainstream option. ÖBB has said demand on its night network has grown, though the Paris-Berlin route remains subject to timetable and fleet planning across several countries.
