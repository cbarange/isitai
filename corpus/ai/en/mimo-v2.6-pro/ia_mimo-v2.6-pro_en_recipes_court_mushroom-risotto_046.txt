Ingredients: 1 cup arborio rice, 3 cups vegetable broth, 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1 small onion, finely chopped, 8 oz sliced mushrooms, 2 garlic cloves, minced, 1/2 cup grated Parmesan, 2 tablespoons butter, salt and pepper. Steps: Sauté onion in oil, add mushrooms and garlic, stir in rice, add broth gradually, simmer until creamy, finish with butter and Parmesan, season.
