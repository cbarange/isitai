Dear partner,

I am writing to inform you of a forthcoming change in one of our key service providers, effective from the first of next month. This communication aims to provide you with a transparent overview of the transition, the rationale behind it, and the measures we have put in place to ensure continuity and minimise any potential disruption to our shared operations.

Following an extensive evaluation of our current service agreements and in line with our commitment to continuous improvement, we have decided to replace our existing supplier for logistics and warehousing services. After a thorough competitive review involving several qualified candidates, we selected a new provider whose profile, capabilities, and strategic objectives align more closely with the direction our partnership is taking. The decision was reached after careful consideration of quality benchmarks, operational reliability, cost efficiency, and long-term scalability — all criteria that directly support the stability and growth of our collaboration.

We wish to emphasise that this change is purely internal to our supply chain and does not alter the terms, conditions, or service levels of our existing partnership agreement. Your account management team, point of contact, and contractual commitments remain entirely unchanged. We recognise, however, that any modification within our operational ecosystem may have downstream implications, and we believe it is important to keep you fully informed so that you can plan accordingly.

The transition will be executed in phases to mitigate risk. During the first two weeks, the new provider will be integrated into our systems and processes while the outgoing supplier completes the handover of all active shipments, inventory records, and operational documentation. We have established a dedicated task force internally to oversee this period and to address any unforeseen challenges immediately. Should any inconsistency arise during this window, we will notify you without delay and provide a corrective action plan within twenty-four hours.

In terms of concrete benefits for our partnership, we anticipate the following improvements:
Enhanced delivery reliability. The new provider operates from a broader network of distribution centres, which should result in shorter lead times and more consistent delivery windows across all territories.
Improved traceability. Their tracking infrastructure is more advanced, offering real-time updates that we will be able to share with you through our standard reporting channels.
Greater flexibility. Their capacity model allows for more agile adjustments to volume fluctuations, which is particularly relevant given the seasonal patterns we both observe.
Stronger sustainability credentials. Their fleet and warehouse operations comply with the latest environmental standards, reinforcing our shared commitment to responsible business practices.

We will be scheduling a briefing call in the coming days to walk you through the transition timeline in greater detail and to address any questions or concerns you may have. A calendar invitation will follow shortly, and we encourage you to share it with any members of your team who may find it relevant. In the meantime, should you wish to discuss this matter sooner, please do not hesitate to reach out directly to your usual account manager, who has been fully briefed on all aspects of the change.

We value the trust you place in us and take our responsibility to maintain transparent communication very seriously. Transitions of this nature are not entered into lightly, and we are confident that this move will strengthen our operational foundation and support the continued success of our partnership. We are committed to keeping you informed at every stage and to making this process as seamless as possible for all parties involved.

Thank you for your understanding and continued collaboration. We look forward to our upcoming conversation and to the positive outcomes this transition will bring to both our organisations.
