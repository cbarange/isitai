First Meridian Bancorp and Cascade Trust announced a $4.2 billion merger Tuesday, creating the nation's eighth-largest bank by deposits. The combined institution will hold roughly $380 billion in assets across 1,900 branches in 26 states. Executives said the deal, expected to close in the third quarter pending regulatory approval, would generate $600 million in annual cost savings. Consumer advocates urged regulators to scrutinize potential branch closures in rural markets.
