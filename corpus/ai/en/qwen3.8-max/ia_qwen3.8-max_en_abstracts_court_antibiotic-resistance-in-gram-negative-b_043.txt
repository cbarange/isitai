Background: Antibiotic resistance in Gram-negative bacteria threatens global health. Methods: We reviewed surveillance data and resistance mechanisms, including beta-lactamases and efflux pumps. Results: Carbapenem and third-generation cephalosporin resistance increased across Enterobacterales and Pseudomonas, driven by plasmid-mediated genes and treatment failures. Conclusion: Enhanced surveillance, antimicrobial stewardship, and novel therapies are urgently needed.
