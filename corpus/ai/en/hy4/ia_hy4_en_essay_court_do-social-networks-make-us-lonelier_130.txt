Nowadays, social networks connect millions of people, but many users still feel isolated. Do they make us lonelier?

In my view, they can. Online conversations often replace face-to-face meetings, and comparing our lives with perfect photos creates jealousy and insecurity. However, networks also help shy or distant people find support.

Therefore, social networks do not automatically cause loneliness; their effect depends on how we use them. Used wisely, they complete real relationships rather than replace them.
