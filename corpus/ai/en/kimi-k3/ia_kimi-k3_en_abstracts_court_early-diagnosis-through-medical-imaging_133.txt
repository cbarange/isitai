Background: Early diagnosis significantly improves patient outcomes, and medical imaging is central to detecting diseases at treatable stages. Methods: This review examines recent advances in MRI, CT, PET, ultrasound, and AI-assisted image analysis for early disease detection. Results: Modern imaging identifies tumors, neurodegeneration, and cardiovascular abnormalities at preclinical stages with increasing sensitivity and specificity. Conclusion: Medical imaging, enhanced by artificial intelligence, is transforming early diagnosis, enabling timely intervention and improved prognosis.
