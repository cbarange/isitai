**Ingredients**

For the crust:
- 1 9-inch pie crust (homemade or store-bought)

For the lemon filling:
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1½ cups (350 ml) water
- ½ cup fresh lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 4 egg yolks, lightly beaten

For the meringue:
- 4 egg whites, at room temperature
- 6 tablespoons granulated sugar
- ¼ teaspoon cream of tartar
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

**Instructions**

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Bake the pie crust for 12–15 minutes until golden, then let it cool slightly.

2. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the sugar, cornstarch, flour, and salt. Gradually stir in the water, lemon juice, and zest. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and comes to a boil.

3. Slowly whisk about half of the hot mixture into the beaten egg yolks, then pour everything back into the saucepan. Cook for 2 more minutes, stirring constantly. Remove from the heat and stir in the butter until smooth. Pour the filling into the baked crust.

4. For the meringue, beat the egg whites with the cream of tartar until foamy. Gradually add the sugar, beating until stiff, glossy peaks form. Mix in the vanilla.

5. Spread the meringue over the hot filling, sealing it carefully to the edges of the crust to prevent shrinking.

6. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the meringue is golden brown.

7. Cool at room temperature for 1 hour, then refrigerate for at least 3 hours before slicing and serving.
