Dear [Supplier Name] Support Team,

I am writing to formally report a technical incident involving the Model XT-450 pressure sensors supplied under Purchase Order #PO-78234, delivered to our facility on March 12, 2026. Over the past ten days, we have experienced recurring malfunctions with a significant portion of these units, resulting in operational disruptions and production delays. We request your urgent assistance in investigating this matter and identifying an appropriate resolution.

**1. Incident Overview**
The issue was first identified on April 1, 2026, at approximately 08:30 AM, when our monitoring systems flagged abnormal pressure readings on Production Line B. Upon further inspection, our maintenance team determined that several XT-450 sensors were transmitting intermittent and inconsistent data. Since the initial occurrence, similar malfunctions have been observed across multiple units installed on Production Lines A, B, and C.

To date, we have identified 18 affected sensors out of the 50 units delivered in this shipment. The affected units correspond to the following batch and serial number range: Batch #XT450-26-03, Serial Numbers SN-1041 through SN-1090. While not all units in this range have failed, the frequency and consistency of the issues suggest a potential systemic problem with this batch.

**2. Observed Symptoms**
The malfunctions have manifested in the following ways:
- **Intermittent signal loss:** Sensors periodically cease transmitting data for intervals ranging from 30 seconds to 5 minutes before resuming operation without manual intervention.
- **Inaccurate readings:** Several sensors have reported pressure values deviating by 15% to 25% from verified baseline measurements, as confirmed by our calibrated reference instruments.
- **Unprompted resets:** A subset of the affected units has undergone spontaneous resets during normal operating conditions, disrupting continuous monitoring processes.

These symptoms have occurred under standard operating conditions, well within the temperature, pressure, and humidity parameters specified in the product documentation.

**3. Operational Impact**
The instability of these sensors has had a measurable impact on our operations. Production Line B experienced a cumulative downtime of approximately six hours over the past week while our team conducted diagnostics and implemented temporary manual monitoring procedures. Additionally, the unreliable readings have required us to halt two production runs as a precautionary measure to ensure product quality and workplace safety. While we have implemented interim workarounds, these are not sustainable and continue to place additional strain on our maintenance and quality assurance teams.

**4. Troubleshooting Steps Taken**
Our technical team has undertaken the following steps to isolate the cause of the issue:
- Verified that all sensors were installed in accordance with the provided installation guidelines.
- Inspected wiring, power supplies, and network connections to rule out external infrastructure issues.
- Updated the firmware on all affected units to the latest version available on your support portal.
- Recalibrated the sensors using our in-house calibration equipment.
- Replaced two of the malfunctioning units with spare XT-450 sensors from a previous shipment (Batch #XT450-25-11), which have since operated without issue.

Despite these measures, the problems persist in the units from Batch #XT450-26-03, leading us to believe the root cause may be related to a manufacturing or component defect specific to this batch.
