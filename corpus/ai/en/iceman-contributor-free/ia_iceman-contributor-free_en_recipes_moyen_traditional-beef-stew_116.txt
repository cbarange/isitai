**Ingredients:**
- 900 g (2 lbs) beef chuck, cut into 3 cm cubes
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 950 ml (4 cups) beef broth
- 240 ml (1 cup) dry red wine
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 3 potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 2 celery stalks, sliced
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish

**Steps:**
1. **Prepare the beef:** In a bowl, toss the beef cubes with flour, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
2. **Brown the meat:** Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in batches for about 5 minutes per side, then set aside.
3. **Sauté the aromatics:** In the same pot, cook the onion and celery for 5 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and tomato paste, stirring for 1 minute.
