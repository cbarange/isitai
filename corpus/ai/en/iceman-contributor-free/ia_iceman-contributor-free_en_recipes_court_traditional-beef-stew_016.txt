**Ingredients:**
- 500 g beef, cubed
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 potatoes, diced
- 1 onion, chopped
- 500 ml beef broth
- 2 tbsp flour
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt and pepper

**Steps:**
1. Heat oil in a pot and brown the beef. Add the onion and cook until soft.
2. Stir in the flour, then pour in the broth.
3. Add carrots and potatoes, season with salt and pepper.
4. Cover and simmer for 1.5 hours until the beef is tender.
