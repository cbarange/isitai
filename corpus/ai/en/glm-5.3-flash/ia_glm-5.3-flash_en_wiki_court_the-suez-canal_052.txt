The Suez Canal is an artificial sea-level waterway in Egypt, connecting the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea through the Isthmus of Suez. Opened in November 1869 after a decade of construction led by the French Suez Canal Company under Ferdinand de Lesseps, it allows vessels to travel between Europe and South Asia without circumnavigating Africa. The canal, extended and deepened several times, remains one of the world's busiest shipping routes and a vital source of Egyptian revenue.
