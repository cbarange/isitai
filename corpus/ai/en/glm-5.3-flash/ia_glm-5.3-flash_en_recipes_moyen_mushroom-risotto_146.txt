For the risotto:
- 1 ½ cups Arborio rice
- 300 g mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, or button), sliced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ cup dry white wine
- 5 cups warm vegetable or chicken stock
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

To finish:
- 1 tbsp butter
- Fresh parsley, chopped
- Extra Parmesan for serving

Steps:

1. Heat the stock in a saucepan and keep it warm over low heat.

2. In a large pan, heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter. Sauté the mushrooms over medium-high heat until golden and their liquid has evaporated, about 6–8 minutes. Season lightly, remove, and set aside.

3. In the same pan, add the remaining oil and butter. Cook the onion over medium heat until soft and translucent, about 4 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.

4. Stir in the rice and toast for 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the grains look slightly translucent at the edges.

5. Pour in the white wine and stir until fully absorbed.

6. Add the warm stock one ladle at a time, stirring frequently. Wait until each ladle is almost fully absorbed before adding the next. Continue for 18–20 minutes, until the rice is creamy and al dente. You may not need all the stock.

7. Stir the cooked mushrooms back into the rice during the last 5 minutes of cooking.

8. Remove from heat. Stir in the Parmesan and the final tablespoon of butter. Season with salt and pepper.

9. Let rest 2 minutes, then garnish with parsley and extra Parmesan. Serve immediately while hot and creamy.
