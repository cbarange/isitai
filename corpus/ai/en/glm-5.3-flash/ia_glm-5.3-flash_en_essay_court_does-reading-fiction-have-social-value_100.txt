Reading fiction, some claim, is mere entertainment. However, I believe it has real social value.

Fiction develops empathy: by living characters' lives, readers understand perspectives different from their own. Moreover, shared stories create cultural references that unite communities and spark debate on social issues.

Admittedly, not every novel transforms its reader. Yet even light fiction exercises imagination and connection.

In conclusion, reading fiction is far from useless; it quietly builds more open, tolerant societies.
