I've had this stand mixer for three months now and honestly, I don't know how I baked without it. The dough hook kneads bread perfectly, and it's so quiet I can run it early in the morning without waking anyone. It's heavy and sturdy, which I love—no wobbling even at high speed. Yes, it's an investment, but the quality is obvious. My only complaint: the bowl is a bit heavy to wash. Still, five stars from me!
