Subject: Organising the Business Trip to Frankfurt – Planning Details and Next Steps

Dear Mr. Harrison,

I hope this message finds you well. With these goals in mind, I have drafted a preliminary schedule and would appreciate your feedback before we proceed with the bookings.

Proposed Itinerary

Monday, 12 May: The group will depart from London St Pancras on the 07:15 Eurostar service to Brussels, connecting to a direct high-speed train to Frankfurt, arriving at approximately 14:40 local time. I have selected rail travel over flights, as it allows the team to work en route, avoids airport transfer times, and aligns with the company's sustainability commitments outlined in last year's corporate responsibility report. Upon arrival, the team will check in at the Hotel Victoria, located in the city centre and within walking distance of both the conference venue and Schneider & Vogt's offices. The remainder of the afternoon is reserved for rest and preparation, with an optional informal dinner at 19:30 for those who wish to attend.

Tuesday, 13 May: The full day is dedicated to the European Trade Partners Conference. Ms. Okafor will deliver our presentation on market expansion strategy at 11:00, and the afternoon sessions include two workshops on supply chain resilience that are directly relevant to the warehouse visit. I recommend that all four of us attend the morning plenary, after which we can divide the workshops between us to maximise coverage of the sessions. The conference dinner, which requires confirmation by 25 April, offers a valuable networking opportunity, and I would suggest that you and Ms. Okafor attend on behalf of the team.

Wednesday, 14 May: The morning meeting with Schneider & Vogt GmbH is confirmed for 09:30 at their headquarters and is expected to last approximately three hours, including a working lunch. Mr. Delacroix has prepared the draft negotiation brief, and I will circulate it to the group by the end of next week so that we can review our positions together during the train journey on Monday. In the afternoon, we are scheduled to tour the regional warehouse at 15:00, followed by a debrief with the site manager. Given the distance between the two locations, I have arranged for a hire car and driver for that day only, which is more cost-effective than multiple taxi journeys.

Thursday, 15 May: The morning is set aside for internal review: a two-hour session to consolidate our notes from the conference, agree on follow-up actions regarding the Schneider & Vogt negotiations, and assign responsibilities for the post-trip report. The team will then take the 13:20 train back, arriving in London by approximately 20:30.

This figure falls comfortably within the budget approved for the quarter. I would like to place the bookings by Friday, 25 April, in order to secure the early-bird conference rate and the guaranteed hotel availability; therefore, I would be grateful for your approval by Wednesday, 23 April at the latest.
