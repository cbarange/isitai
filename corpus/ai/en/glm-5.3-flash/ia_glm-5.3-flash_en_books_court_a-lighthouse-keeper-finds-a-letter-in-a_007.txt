The tide left its offering at dawn: green glass, corked and salt-scabbed, half-buried in kelp. Merrin knelt, palms raw from trimming the wick, and drew the letter out like a splinter from the sea's flesh. The paper was dry—impossible—and the ink unfaded. "To whoever still keeps a light," it began, "we saw yours falter last winter. Hold on. We are holding too."
