Ingredients: 1 lb beef cubes, 2 tablespoons flour, 2 tablespoons oil, 1 onion chopped, 2 carrots sliced, 2 potatoes cubed, 2 cups beef broth, 1 teaspoon salt, 1/2 teaspoon pepper, 2 bay leaves.
Steps: Toss beef with flour. Brown in oil; remove. Sauté onion, add beef, broth, seasonings, and bay. Simmer 45 minutes. Add carrots and potatoes; cook 30 minutes until tender. Remove bay leaves and serve.
