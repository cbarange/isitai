Okay so I literally just got back from this hike and I'm still not over it?? Like at all?? 🤩

I've been wanting to do this trail for MONTHS and I finally made it happen yesterday morning and honestly? It exceeded every single expectation I had. The photo I just posted does NOT do it justice but I tried my best 😅📸

So here's the thing — I woke up at like 5am which is completely unlike me because I am NOT a morning person, like at all, but the sunrise was calling and I couldn't say no. The drive up was so peaceful, just me, my playlist, and an excessive amount of coffee ☕🚗

When I first got to the trailhead I almost turned back because the sky looked a little cloudy and I was like "well there goes the views" BUT it actually ended up being perfect because the clouds created this crazy dramatic effect over the peaks later in the day. Nature really said "trust me on this one" ⛰️☁️

The hike itself was no joke though?? My legs are screaming at me today lol. There was this one section near the top where I genuinely questioned all my life choices and why I thought this was fun 😂 But then I turned around and saw how far I'd already come and it was like... wow. Like physically and metaphorically wow, you know?

What really got me was the quiet up there. Not gonna get all philosophical on you but when you're standing above the tree line with nothing but wind and rocks and sky, your brain just kind of... shuts up? All the overthinking, the to-do lists, the random anxieties — gone. Just gone. It's like nature's reset button 🧠✨🌬️

I met this couple near the summit who were celebrating their anniversary by hiking which I thought was adorable?? We took photos for each other and they told me they've been doing a big hike every year since they got married. Ten years! I love that so much 🥹❤️

Also saw a marmot and I'm pretty sure we had a moment. He looked at me, I looked at him, we understood each other on a spiritual level 🐿️ (okay maybe that's a stretch but he was cute)

The way back down my knees were NOT happy with me but whatever, suffering is temporary and memories are forever right?? Or at least that's what I kept telling myself while hobbling down those switchbacks 😭🦵

If anyone's been thinking about doing a hike like this — DO IT. Seriously. Stop waiting for the "right time" or the perfect gear or the ideal weather day. Just go. Wear the shoes you have. Bring the snacks you like. Start before you feel ready because you never will 🎒👟

Some tips from someone who learned the hard way:
Bring MORE water than you think you need, seriously, double it 💧
Sunscreen even when it's cloudy, the sun up there is NOT playing around ☀️
Pack a jacket because the weather changes in literally minutes 🧥
Tell someone where you're going, safety first always 🙏
Take the photo but also put the phone away and just LOOK, like really look 👀
