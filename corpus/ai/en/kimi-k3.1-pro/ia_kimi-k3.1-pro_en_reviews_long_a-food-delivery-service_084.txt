★★★★☆ — Convenient, reliable, and customer support actually helped

I’ve been using this food delivery service for almost a year now, mostly on Friday evenings and on weekdays when work runs late and cooking feels impossible. I didn’t expect to keep the app installed this long, but it has become one of the few I genuinely use every week. It isn’t perfect, and the extra costs mean I can’t justify ordering all the time, but my overall experience has been positive enough that I wanted to leave a proper review rather than just a star rating.

The ordering process is the part I appreciate most. The app is easy to navigate, the menus are usually clear, and reordering something I’ve had before takes less than a minute. I also like being able to save several addresses and payment methods without having to enter everything again. The estimated arrival times have generally been realistic for me. When the app says forty minutes, it usually means somewhere around forty minutes, not an unexplained hour and a half. Being able to follow the courier on the map is useful too, especially because I live in an apartment building and like to meet them near the entrance instead of making them search for my door.

Most of my orders have arrived warm, complete, and in good condition. Special instructions, such as leaving out an ingredient or including extra utensils, have been followed more often than not. I’ve also discovered a couple of small local restaurants through the app that I probably wouldn’t have tried otherwise. One family-run place in particular has become a favorite, and I now order directly from them when I can. In that sense, the service has been useful beyond simple convenience.

That said, there have been problems. On one order, my main dish was missing and the drinks had tipped over inside the bag. I was expecting the usual frustrating conversation with an automated support system, but reporting the issue was straightforward. I uploaded two photos, explained what had happened, and received a response much faster than I expected. The missing item was refunded, and I was given a small credit for the rest. I would obviously have preferred to receive the correct dinner in the first place, but mistakes happen. What matters to me is that I didn’t have to spend my evening arguing to get a reasonable solution.

Another order arrived nearly an hour late during bad weather. The delay itself was understandable, but the lack of updates was annoying because the estimate barely changed until the food was almost here. Better communication during delays would make a big difference. Even a short message saying that the restaurant was behind schedule would allow customers to plan instead of repeatedly checking the map.

My main hesitation is the price. Once the delivery fee, service fee, and tip are added, a meal can cost considerably more than expected. The small-order fee on some restaurants also makes it expensive for one person. I understand that couriers and restaurants need to be paid fairly, so I’m not asking for everything to be free, but I would appreciate clearer totals earlier in the process and fewer promotions that look generous until you read all the conditions. I now check the final amount carefully before placing an order, and sometimes I decide to cook instead.

The couriers I’ve met have almost all been polite, and several have gone out of their way to follow delivery instructions. I hope the service treats them well, because their work is a huge part of the experience. Customers see the restaurant and the app, but the person bringing the food is often the one dealing with traffic, weather, confusing addresses, and tight schedules.

Overall, this is a service I’m comfortable recommending, with the understanding that it works best as an occasional convenience rather than an everyday habit. The app is dependable, the selection is good, and my most serious problem was handled fairly. Better delay notifications and more transparent pricing would easily earn the fifth star from me. For now, I’ll keep using it—just not so often that my budget starts complaining louder than my stomach.
