Sur le plan de l’emploi, 35 postes de responsables et d’ingénieurs seront pourvus en priorité during the first year, aux côtés de technicians de maintenance, administrateurs de systèmes et agents de sécurité. A training programme is being conducted with the University of Bordeaux, the technological institute of Bordeaux and several vocational schools. The company has also announced the opening of a university research chair focused on the thermal management of high-density computing infrastructure.

Le site a été conçu according to a modular architecture permitting the progressive addition of computing capacity. Additional investment of 120 millions d’euros could be made over the next five years if demand is confirmed. Novahertz has already signed agreements with several European financial institutions, public operators and healthcare groups. Les données clients seront hébergées in France and in accordance with the applicable European regulations, a précisé l’entreprise.

During the inauguration, elected officials visited the installation’s control room. Several delegates were shown real-time monitoring of the cooling systems, the electrical network and the site’s energy consumption. The first commercial clients are expected to begin transferring data in November, while the initial production phase has been scheduled for the beginning of 2026.

The opening also marks the consolidation of the Gironde’s ambitions in the digital infrastructure sector. According to the regional development agency, the department now hosts six data centres and nearly 900 people directly employed by operators and specialised maintenance companies. Regional authorities have announced the creation of a “digital infrastructure task force” to coordinate the development of sites, training and energy requirements.

For residents and local authorities, the principal issues remain the transparency of the projects, their concrete contribution to the local economy and their compatibility with the environmental constraints of the territory. Novahertz estimates that the first full year of operation will generate 4.6 million euros in local taxes and business contributions. The company has also pledged to fund the renovation of a bus stop, the extension of a cycle path and part of the lighting works in the industrial zone.

L’inauguration was followed by the presentation of the site to approximately 200 guests, including elected officials, business representatives, university researchers and operators from the prospective clients. A second opening ceremony is planned next month for a “public operations centre”, which will house teams responsible for monitoring the platforms hosted in Novabordeaux and for coordinating maintenance with the various operators.
