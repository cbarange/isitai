Background: Global climate models often lack the spatial resolution required for assessing local climate impacts. Methods: We employed a high-resolution regional climate model (RCM) to dynamically downscale global projections over the Mediterranean basin for the 2050–2100 period. Results: Simulations reveal a 25% increase in extreme summer precipitation events and a 3°C rise in mean temperatures compared to baseline data. Conclusion: High-resolution regional modelling is essential for capturing localized extremes and informing targeted climate adaptation strategies.
