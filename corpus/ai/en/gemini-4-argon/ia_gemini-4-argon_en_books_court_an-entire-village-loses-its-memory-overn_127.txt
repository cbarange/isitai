Dawn broke over Oakhaven, illuminating a town of strangers. The baker stared at his flour-dusted hands, weeping for a craft he no longer recognized. Husbands and wives shared pillows, waking to gaze into eyes suddenly hollowed of history. Photographs on mantlepieces smiled back as mocking enigmas. Outside, villagers wandered the cobblestone streets, whispering prayers to forgotten gods, mourning the ghosts of themselves that the night had silently stolen away.
