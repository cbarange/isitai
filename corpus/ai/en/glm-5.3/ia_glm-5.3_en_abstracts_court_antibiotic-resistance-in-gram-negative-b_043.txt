Background: Antibiotic resistance in Gram-negative bacteria is escalating worldwide, limiting therapeutic options.

Methods: We analyzed 500 clinical isolates from three hospitals, performing susceptibility testing and PCR screening for resistance genes.

Results: Sixty-two percent of isolates were multidrug-resistant; blaCTX-M and blaNDM predominated, correlating with carbapenem resistance.

Conclusion: Enhanced surveillance and antimicrobial stewardship are urgently needed to curb resistance spread.
