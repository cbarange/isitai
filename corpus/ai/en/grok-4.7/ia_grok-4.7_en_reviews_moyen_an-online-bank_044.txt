I've been with this online bank for a little over a year now, and I wanted to share my experience because choosing where to keep your money is never a small decision. I switched from a traditional branch bank after getting tired of fees and limited hours, and so far I'm glad I made the move.

Opening the account took about fifteen minutes on my phone. The app is clear, transfers between my accounts are instant, and I get a notification every time something moves. Direct deposit landed on time from the first paycheck, and the debit card arrived in less than a week. Customer support has been better than I expected for a bank without branches: I used the chat twice, once about a declined card while traveling, and both times someone answered within a few minutes and actually solved the problem.

What I appreciate most is the transparency. There are no monthly fees if you meet the simple requirements, and I can see pending charges the same day. Savings buckets helped me set aside money for rent and a small emergency fund without opening extra accounts.

It isn't perfect. Cash deposits are awkward if you don't have a partner ATM nearby, and I still miss being able to walk into a branch for the occasional complicated question. The interest rate on savings is decent but not the highest I've seen advertised.

Even so, for everyday banking I feel more in control than I did before. I'd recommend it to anyone who is comfortable managing money from their phone and doesn't need a teller every week.
